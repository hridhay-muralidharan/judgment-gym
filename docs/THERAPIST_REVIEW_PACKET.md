# Therapist review packet — Judgment Gym 2.0

## Purpose

This packet is for expert feedback on the product concept, its self-model, its safety boundaries, and one user-generated output. It is not a request for a diagnosis, treatment plan, or endorsement of clinical efficacy.

## What to review

Please review the attached or locally generated:

1. the product overview;
2. the self-model research basis;
3. the model output generated from the user's autobiographical material;
4. the “Show me about yourself” interview output;
5. the safety and privacy boundaries.

The raw autobiographical material should be shared separately and only through a channel the user considers appropriate. It is intentionally not stored in this public repository.

## Requested rating

Please select one:

- Judgment Gym is not solving any meaningful problem today or a potential problem of the future.
- The problem has been correctly identified, but the solution appears to be in the wrong direction.
- The problem has been correctly identified and the solution is moving in the right direction, but needs further refinement and iteration for a viable v1.
- The problem has been correctly identified and the solution is moving in the right direction and sufficient for a viable v1.

## Please add the next-step condition

What is the most important thing that would need to be true for this to move one level higher?

## Open feedback prompts

- Does the output distinguish observation from interpretation clearly enough?
- Does any wording sound diagnostic, pathologizing, overly certain, or reductive?
- Does the model preserve ambiguity and competing explanations?
- Are the selected lenses appropriate for a non-clinical reflection product?
- Could any prompt increase rumination, shame, dependence, or emotional activation?
- What should never be generated without a clinician or user explicitly introducing it?
- Would this output help a therapy conversation, or create more work for the therapist?
- What would make the tool feel respectful across different cultures, families, and therapy approaches?
- What privacy or sharing boundary is currently too weak?

## Current boundary

Judgment Gym is a self-awareness and judgment-reflection tool. It is not a therapist, diagnostic tool, risk assessor, crisis service, treatment recommender, or substitute for clinical care. The user remains the authority on their experience. Model interpretations are hypotheses that can be corrected, qualified, rejected, or left unresolved.
