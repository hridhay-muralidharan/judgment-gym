# Self-awareness, human outcomes, and the GenAI opportunity

## Research direction

The working product is designed to improve self-awareness. The research programme will identify the forms of self-awareness that matter and connect them to outcomes people care about.

Self-awareness may include noticing feelings and body responses, understanding thoughts and assumptions, recognising wants and values, observing behaviour and consequences, and monitoring confidence and uncertainty. Each dimension needs its own evidence review.

## A natural fit: self-awareness and judgment

Becker et al. (2023) conducted a process-tracing experiment in which participants repeatedly completed a five-step planning task. Participants who answered structured questions about how they had made their decisions discovered adaptive, far-sighted strategies faster than participants who answered unrelated questions or moved directly to the next decision.

This is a natural fit for the product's reflection-and-situation loop. It supports the idea that structured reflection on how a person reached a judgment can improve the learning of decision strategies in a bounded task.

Source: [Systematic metacognitive reflection helps people discover far-sighted decision strategies](https://doi.org/10.1017/jdm.2023.16).

Sunderaraman et al. (2020) show why awareness needs to include calibration. In financial decision-making tasks, overconfidence was associated with lower accuracy. The study measured awareness through calibration and resolution: whether confidence tracks actual performance.

Source: [Self-awareness for financial decision-making abilities in healthy adults](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0235558).

## Outcomes to investigate

The research review will examine whether improved self-awareness supports:

- emotional regulation;
- decision-making and judgment;
- self-regulation and behaviour change;
- values-aligned action and life direction;
- communication and relationships;
- better preparation for therapy and coaching conversations.

Each outcome will be included only when the construct, population, intervention, and measured outcome are a natural fit. Correlation, intervention evidence, and theory will be clearly separated. A promising connection that remains early or indirect will be labelled as a hypothesis.

## Product hypothesis

> Structured reflection on lived situations can improve self-awareness. Better self-awareness may help people regulate emotions, make decisions, change unhelpful patterns, act in line with their values, and relate to others more effectively.

GenAI creates an opportunity to make this reflection interactive, personal, continuous, and available across many situations. The system can ask situation-specific questions, retain a user-correctable history, generate a related situation, and help the person compare what changed.

The primary product outcome is improved self-awareness. Judgment quality is one possible downstream outcome, supported by the Becker and Sunderaraman findings. Other outcomes will be added when the evidence fits naturally.

## Evaluation implications

The product should eventually be evaluated on:

- clarity about feelings, thoughts, wants, values, and behaviour;
- ability to identify assumptions and uncertainty;
- calibration between confidence and observable task performance where ground truth exists;
- quality of alternative explanations;
- strategy change after structured reflection;
- transfer from constructed situations back to lived situations;
- action taken in line with chosen values or goals;
- communication and therapy-conversation usefulness;
- indicators of rumination, distress, shame, overconfidence, or dependence.

The Becker study supports testing decision-strategy learning. It does not validate a mental-health intervention or a longitudinal self-model by itself. Those claims require new studies.

## Physical gym analogy

The physical gym can remain a motivating analogy for deliberate practice. Its relevance will depend on the final research and product definition. It does not decide the product name or the central outcome.

## Naming remains open

“Judgment Gym” is a working title. It will remain only if judgment continues to describe the central practice and human value after the self-awareness research review.
