# Target audience and market analysis

## Target audience

Room to Respond is for therapists, life coaches, and clients who already use technology for mental health, wellbeing, coaching, journaling, meditation, habits, learning, or personal development.

The practitioner is the buyer and routine creator. The client is the person who practises between sessions and shares a report. Digital mental-health, therapy, coaching, and wellbeing companies are potential distribution partners.

The audience is global. Country is used to show market contribution and prioritise research.

## TAM and SAM

### TAM — people who seek mental-health services

TAM is the global population that seeks mental-health services. This includes people who seek support from therapists, psychologists, psychiatrists, counsellors, coaches, community services, and other mental-health providers.

TAM is a people-based market definition. It is measured by the number of people seeking services in a defined year, with country-level counts added to a global total.

The [WHO World Mental Health surveys](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2847360/) provide cross-country evidence on mental-health service use. The [WHO Mental Health Atlas](https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/data-research/mental-health-atlas) provides country-level information on mental-health systems and service availability. These sources provide the starting point for building the TAM dataset.

### SAM — people who use technology-based mental-health solutions

SAM is the subset of TAM that has adopted technology-based mental-health solutions beyond virtual consultation. This includes mental-health apps, digital therapeutics, guided digital interventions, digital journaling, structured wellbeing programmes, and AI-supported mental-health products.

SAM is also measured by people, not revenue. A person enters SAM when they use one of these products during the defined measurement period.

The [global survey of digital mental-health implementation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41347-024-00447-1) provides evidence on the availability and use of digital mental-health tools across world regions. Country-level user counts for the specific SAM definition require additional market, platform, and primary research.

## Country contribution view

The market view will show two charts:

1. **TAM contribution:** the share of global mental-health service seekers contributed by the five largest countries, with all remaining countries grouped as “Other countries”.
2. **SAM contribution:** the share of global technology-based mental-health users contributed by the five largest countries, with all remaining countries grouped as “Other countries”.

Each chart will use:

```text
country share = country people in segment / global people in segment
```

The country ranking will be based on the segment being measured. TAM ranking will use people seeking mental-health services. SAM ranking will use people using technology-based solutions beyond virtual consultation.

The repository will add the pie charts after the country-level counts use compatible definitions, years, and population bases. Current research provides the measurement framework and partial country coverage; it does not provide one complete global SAM dataset.

## Market-sizing work

The next research pass should:

- define the measurement year;
- assemble country-level TAM counts from comparable service-use sources;
- assemble country-level SAM counts from digital mental-health usage sources, platform data, surveys, and primary research;
- remove virtual consultation from the SAM definition;
- rank countries by contribution;
- group the remaining countries as “Other countries”;
- publish source, year, definition, and calculation beside each chart.

The market analysis will show the size and geographic concentration of the audience. Practitioner interviews and client research will then test whether this audience wants practitioner-created mental workout routines and whether the reports improve the next session.
