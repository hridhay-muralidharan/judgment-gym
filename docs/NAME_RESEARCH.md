# Product naming research

## Naming brief

The product needs a name for a personal practice system that helps people develop the faculties they use to live. It starts with real situations and uses text, voice, reflection, scenario variation, and longitudinal memory to support practice.

The name should:

- describe a meaningful human benefit without making a clinical promise;
- leave room for many faculties and workout areas;
- feel thoughtful and serious enough for mental-health AI companies;
- feel human enough for people to use personally;
- support text, voice, simulated situations, and a privacy-first future;
- be memorable without sounding like a generic wellness app;
- remain useful if the product becomes infrastructure inside a larger mental-health platform.

The current name is not protected by sunk cost. It is one candidate among many.

## Initial market scan

Several obvious names are already strongly associated with adjacent products:

- **Reflection** — an established AI journaling and coaching product with text, voice, prompts, and longitudinal insights. [Reflection](https://www.reflection.app/)
- **Human Practice** — an existing wellbeing and therapy practice. [The Human Practice](https://myhumanpractice.com/)
- **Inner Atlas** — existing mental-health reflection and mapping products. [Inner Atlas](https://inneratlas.app/)
- **Mental Fitness** — an existing mental-fitness platform. [NUE](https://talkwithnue.com/)
- **Mental Reps** — an existing mental-performance app. [Mental Reps](https://www.mymentalreps.com/)
- **Bearing** — existing emotional journaling, mindfulness, and AI orientation products. [Bearing](https://getbearingapp.com/), [Bearing AI](https://mybearing.app/)
- **The Practice** — an existing app and several therapy or wellbeing practices use the phrase. [The Practice](https://practiceapp.org/)
- **Mirror** — an existing Child Mind Institute reflection app. [Mirror](https://childmind.org/science/applied-technologies/mirror/)
- **Reflexion** — an adjacent reflective AI project explicitly focused on structured reflection, capacity-building, and judgment. [Reflexion](https://www.reflexionai.dev/)

These names are useful category signals. They are poor starting points for a new product name because they create immediate confusion or weak distinctiveness.

## Candidate territories

### 1. Space before response

**Room to Respond** is the strongest current candidate in this territory. It describes the human benefit clearly: more space to notice, understand, and choose a response. It supports emotional regulation, judgment, communication, and flexibility without choosing one faculty as the whole product.

Strength: clear, human, and connected to the lived-situation loop.

Risk: longer than a conventional one-word brand and may sound more like a programme title.

### 2. Range of response

**Repertoire** describes the set of responses, interpretations, and strategies a person can draw on. The gym model becomes a way to expand a person's repertoire across situations.

Strength: broad, substantive, and closely connected to development.

Risk: existing music-practice products use the name and the word may feel academic. [Repertoire](https://repertoirehq.com/)

### 3. Deliberate consideration

**Consider** is simple and relates to attention, perspective, judgment, and choice.

Strength: familiar word with a useful verb form.

Risk: broad and difficult to own as a distinctive product name.

### 4. Practice in real life

**Fieldwork** describes learning from lived situations and returning insights to the field of life.

Strength: grounded, active, and compatible with research.

Risk: already crowded in business software and may sound like a research or operations product. [Fieldwork AI](https://www.getfieldwork.ai/)

### 5. Human faculties

**The Faculty** or **Faculties** directly expresses the research structure.

Strength: closely aligned with the first-principles map.

Risk: academic, institutional, and already used in education and fitness products. [The Faculty](https://www.thefaculty.ai/)

## Current recommendation

The strongest semantic candidate from this first pass is **Room to Respond**.

It captures the core experience without narrowing the product to self-awareness, judgment, therapy, or one mental faculty. It also gives the product a clear promise:

> Create more room between what happens and what you do next.

That promise can contain different workouts for emotion, attention, memory, perspective, judgment, values, relationships, flexibility, and learning.

This is an initial naming recommendation, not a final clearance. The next step is to test “Room to Respond” against the current product storyboard with the therapist, target companies, and potential users, then check domains, repository availability, and trademark risk before renaming anything.

## Decision rule

The final name should win because it best communicates the product and survives basic market checks. Existing repository files, URLs, or the current visual identity do not count in its favour.
