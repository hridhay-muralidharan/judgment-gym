# Therapist review packet — Room to Respond 2.0

## Purpose

This packet requests expert feedback on a product concept and one user-generated exploration. It is not a request for diagnosis, treatment, or clinical endorsement.

## What to review

Please review:

1. one lived-situation capture;
2. the model's tentative observations and evidence;
3. the reason for the selected probe;
4. the constructed scenario and the user's response;
5. the transfer-back questions and model correction;
6. the privacy, safety, and future on-device AI thesis.

## Requested rating

- Room to Respond is not solving any meaningful problem today or a potential problem of the future.
- The problem has been correctly identified, but the solution appears to be in the wrong direction.
- The problem has been correctly identified and the solution is moving in the right direction, but needs further refinement and iteration for a viable v1.
- The problem has been correctly identified and the solution is moving in the right direction and sufficient for a viable v1.

Please add: “What would need to be true for this assessment to move one level higher?”

## Open feedback

- Does the lived-situation capture help distinguish event, feeling, thought, speech, action, want, and consequence?
- Are the model's observations grounded and easy to correct?
- Does the model preserve competing explanations and context?
- Is the selected constructed scenario psychologically bounded and non-leading?
- Could the simulation increase rumination, shame, activation, or dependence?
- Does transfer back to lived experience create useful reflection or unnecessary interpretation?
- What should never be generated without the user or clinician explicitly introducing it?
- Would this material help a therapy conversation or create more work for the therapist?
- What privacy or sharing boundary is too weak?

Room to Respond is not a therapist, diagnostic tool, risk assessor, crisis service, treatment recommender, or substitute for care. The user remains the authority on their experience. Model interpretations and constructed probes remain provisional.
